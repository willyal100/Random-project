# Pinterest Kit

Templates are one of Pinterest's biggest niches: people search "budget template," save pins, and buy days later. This kit is ready to post the moment the Gumroad listings are live.

**One-time setup (~15 min):** create a free Pinterest **Business** account → create the three boards below → post pins with the copy in this file, each linking to its Gumroad product page.

**Boards:** `Budget Templates` · `Freelance & Small Business Tools` · `Money Organization`

**Cadence:** 1 pin per day until all 10 are up, then re-pin each to a second relevant board a week later. Pinterest rewards steady posting over bursts. Expect traffic to build over 4–8 weeks — Pinterest is a slow burn that compounds.
