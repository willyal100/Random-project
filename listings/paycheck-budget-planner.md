## Description (paste into Gumroad description)

If you're paid every two weeks, a monthly budget never quite fits — rent comes out of one check, everything else out of the other, and the math lives in your head. This planner works the way you're actually paid.

**How it works**

1. **Setup** — your pay frequency, typical check, and your bills with amounts and due days
2. **Paycheck Planner** — one column per check: enter the date and amount, then assign it — bills first (names pull in automatically), then spending, then savings and extra debt payments
3. **Drive "Left to Assign" to zero** — green zero means every dollar has a job; amber means money is still unassigned; red means you've planned more than the check

Twelve check columns cover about six months of biweekly pay — duplicate the tab and keep going.

**What's inside**

- 💵 Zero-based, paycheck-by-paycheck planning with instant color feedback (green / amber / red)
- 🧾 Bills flow in from Setup automatically — assign each bill to the check that pays it
- 📊 Income vs. assigned chart across all twelve checks
- ✅ Start Here tab with plain-English instructions and two example checks filled in

**Details that matter**

- Works with **any pay rhythm** — weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly — because you set the check dates
- Works in **Microsoft Excel** (2016 or newer) and **Google Sheets** (upload to Drive → Open with Google Sheets)
- **Break-proof:** formula cells are protected so a stray click can't ruin the math — every typing cell is unlocked (unprotect anytime, no password)
- No macros, no plugins, no subscription — instant download

**FAQ**

*I'm paid weekly — does it still work?* Yes. Columns are just checks; enter dates weekly and duplicate the tab more often.

*Is this the same as the Personal Budget Dashboard?* No — that one is a monthly system with annual views, savings goals, and debt payoff. This one is for planning each individual paycheck. They pair well.
