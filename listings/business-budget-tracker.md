## Description (paste into Gumroad description)

"How are we doing against budget?" shouldn't require accounting software or a finance hire. This is the one-file version: plan the year, log what happens, and the dashboard answers the question — with variance in red when a month runs over.

**How it works**

1. **Budget Plan** — one row per category, one column per month. Seasonal budgets welcome: ad spend can spike in Q4, travel can drop in summer. Sixteen customizable categories, ten pre-loaded for a typical small business.
2. **Expenses** — date, vendor, category (dropdown), description, amount. 400 rows.
3. **Revenue** — date, client or source, amount. Keeps the picture honest.
4. **Dashboard** — revenue, expenses, net, and annual budget remaining, then budget vs. actual with **variance by month, by quarter, and by category** — with progress bars and overruns in red.

**Who it's for**

Small businesses, startups, agencies, nonprofits, and side businesses that have outgrown guessing but don't want QuickBooks — plus anyone who needs to hand a clean budget-vs-actual view to a partner, board, or bank.

**Details that matter**

- Works in **Microsoft Excel** (2016 or newer) and **Google Sheets** (upload to Drive → Open with Google Sheets)
- **Break-proof:** formula cells are protected so a stray click can't ruin the math — every cell meant for typing is unlocked (unprotect anytime, no password)
- 🧠 **Auto-written insights** — % of year gone vs % of budget spent, year-end spend projection, largest cost center, and profit kept per revenue dollar
- 🔎 Expense and revenue logs are real Excel tables — sort and filter with one click
- Month-granular budgeting — not one flat number stretched across the year
- Every number is a live formula; the screenshots in this listing are real, unedited exports from the actual file
- No macros, no plugins, no subscription — instant download

*Note: this is a planning aid, not accounting or tax advice.*

**FAQ**

*Can I rename the categories?* Yes — all sixteen. Dropdowns and the dashboard follow your list automatically.

*Multiple years?* Keep logging — the dashboard has a year selector, and the Budget Plan can be updated each January (or duplicate the file per year).
